# Abstract: Harnessing AI in the Heart of Surgery

**Title:** Harnessing AI in the Heart of Surgery: A Practical Primer for the Modern Cardiac Surgeon

**Event:** ACTSSL 2026 Academic Congress
**Presenter:** Dr. MTD Lakshan

---

**Abstract:**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is often perceived by surgeons as a distant future technology involving complex robotics or requiring extensive IT infrastructure. However, the true immediate revolution lies in accessible, low-cost Large Language Models (LLMs) that can be integrated into daily practice today. This presentation, "Harnessing AI in the Heart of Surgery," addresses the critical "integration problem" facing modern cardiac surgeons. It moves beyond theoretical discussions to provide a hands-on, 15-minute primer on utilizing tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google’s NotebookLM to augment surgical decision-making and efficiency.

We will explore practical prompting strategies for automating literature reviews, simplifying complex patient education materials, and acting as a "thinking partner" for differential diagnoses. The session will also demonstrate the power of NotebookLM for grounding AI in a surgeon’s own trusted documents, creating personalized study guides, and streamlining conference preparation. By outlining a feasible daily workflow—from pre-round summaries to evening learning reviews—this talk illustrates how AI can save hours of administrative time, reduce cognitive load, and enhance clinical focus. The session concludes with strategies to avoid common cognitive traps and an actionable plan for immediate adoption, emphasizing that while AI will not replace surgeons, those who master these tools will inevitably outperform those who do not.
