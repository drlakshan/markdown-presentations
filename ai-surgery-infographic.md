# AI in Surgery Infographic

## 🎯 Core Message
**AI augments surgical practice without replacing human judgment**

## 📊 Three Main Sections

### 1. AI Prompting for Surgeons
- **Tools:** ChatGPT/Claude (free)
- **Use Cases:**
  - Literature review & summarization
  - Patient education materials
  - Differential diagnosis thinking
- **Key:** Specific, contextual prompts with evidence requests

### 2. NotebookLM as Research Tool
- **Free Google tool** for document analysis
- **Use Cases:**
  - Conference preparation (10+ hours saved)
  - Literature review for publications
  - Teaching material creation
- **Advantage:** Grounded in your sources, no hallucinations

### 3. Practical Integration
- **Principles:**
  - Start with ONE use case
  - Always validate AI outputs
  - Build into existing workflows
- **Avoid:** Cognitive traps (debt, obesity, inappropriate offloading)

## 🚀 Action Plan Timeline

**Week 1:** Setup accounts (ChatGPT/Claude + NotebookLM)
**Week 2-3:** Daily 10-min practice with one use case
**Week 4:** Add second use case, teach colleague
**Month 2:** Full integration, measure impact

## 📈 Why Now?
- **Exponential adoption curve** (Astro Teller)
- Younger colleagues already using AI
- Patients expect AI-informed care
- Research increasingly AI-assisted

## ✅ Key Takeaways
1. **Accessible NOW** - Free tools, no IT approval needed
2. **Start small** - One use case, 10 minutes daily
3. **AI assists** - You remain the expert surgeon

## 💡 Final Thought
*"The heart of surgery remains human judgment, compassion, and skill. AI simply frees us from computational drudgery to focus on what we do best—heal patients."*
